Frequently Asked Questions

Please reach us at prcc2049@gmail.com if you cannot find an answer to your question.

What are the service times at Pleasant Ridge Christian Church?
Our regular service times are Sundays at 9:00 AM, followed by refreshments at 10 and Sunday School and Small Groups at 10:30 am
What denomination is Pleasant Ridge Christian Church?
We are an independent Christian church affiliated with the Evangelical Association of Reformed and Congregational Churches (EA for short). Our beliefs and doctrines align with theirs. For more information, here is a link to their website. https://evangelicalassociation.org/
What types of ministries does Pleasant Ridge Christian Church offer?
We offer a variety of ministries, including worship, children's programs, small groups, outreach, missions, prayer and pastoral care, and more. We will soon be restarting our youth program. Stayed tuned for more
How long are the services?
Our 9 am service is typically about 1 hour. There is coffee, home made goodies and fellowship from 10-10:30 and Small Groups and Sunday School from 10:30-11:30 am
Is there a dress code for attending services at Pleasant Ridge Christian Church?
No, we do not have a specific dress code. We welcome anyone to worship with us in whatever attire they feel comfortable in.
