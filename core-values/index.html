As members of Pleasant Ridge Christian Church, it is our mission to:

(a) Know Jesus Christ as He makes Himself known through the Bible, which is our only rule of faith and practice, and as He makes Himself known to us by the Holy Spirit.

(b) To obey the command of our Lord and to love each other in the same way He loves us.

(c) To obey the Great Commission of our Lord, to reach out to others in His name, to teach them what He commands, and to include them in His church by baptism.

(d) To invite anyone who desires to be part of our fellowship and believes in Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior to join us as we seek to accomplish this mission.

PRCC recognizes the Bible as sufficient rule of faith and practice and holds that living in accordance with the teachings of Jesus Christ is the true test of fellowship. Each member shall have the undisturbed right to follow the word of God according to the dictate of his conscience, under the enlightenment of the Holy Spirit. Pleasant Ridge Christian Church is a member of the Evangelical Association of Reformed Congregational Churches. www.evangelicalassociation.org
