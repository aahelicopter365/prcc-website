Prayer Ministry

Prayer Priority

Prayer is essential to all we do at PRCC. We strive to fulfill the calling of the church to be called a House of Prayer for the nations as Jesus said in Mark 11:17

Not only are all church functions bathed in prayer, PRCC also has a very committed prayer team that prays regularly for local and global prayer needs. Prayer Services are held twice per month

Prayer Meetings

Prayer meetings are held on the 2nd and 4th Tuesdays of each month at 6 pm in church sanctuary - all are welcome
